## 1. The pose source

A [`PoseProvider`](/en/referencia/api/pose-provider/) delivers an `AsyncStream<Pose>`. It can be the camera ([`VisionPoseProvider`](/en/guias/camara-vision/)), a recording ([`RecordedPoseProvider`](/en/guias/pruebas-y-grabaciones/)) or anything you implement. Each [`Pose`](/en/conceptos/pose/) is one frame of body points, in pixels and with sides already corrected.

## 2. The session

[`MotionSession`](/en/referencia/api/motion-session/) is the front door. For every pose:

1. **Visibility.** Are all the joints the exercise measures in view? The change is confirmed after a few frames in a row (~0.25 s) so it doesn't flicker.
2. **Placement.** [`SetupCheck`](/en/guias/calidad-de-medicion/) checks whether the patient faces the camera or stands sideways, is too far away or at the edge of the image.
3. **Engine.** [`RepEngine`](/en/conceptos/repeticiones/) computes the primary measure, smooths it, checks the form rules and decides whether a rep finished.
4. **Baseline.** If one is running, it updates its progress.

Then it publishes the result in two ways.

## 3. Snapshot and events

| | `MotionSnapshot` | `MotionEvent` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | The whole state, in one value | Something that happened on this frame |
| How to read it | `session.snapshot` (observable) | `for await event in session.events()` |
| What for | Drawing: angle, counter, progress ring | Reacting once: say "nice!", vibrate, save |

More detail in [Events and snapshot](/en/guias/eventos-y-snapshot/).

## Exercises are data

The engine has no exercise hard-coded. An [`ExerciseDefinition`](/en/referencia/api/exercise-definition/) describes:

- **what to measure** (`primary`, a [`Measure`](/en/referencia/api/measure/)),
- **how to count** (`kind`: reps or hold),
- **what form to require** (`rules`, a list of [`FormRule`](/en/referencia/api/exercise-definition/#formrule)),
- **where to look from** (`view`: side or front).

Since everything is `Codable`, a new exercise can arrive as JSON from your server. See [Custom exercises](/en/guias/ejercicios-personalizados/).
